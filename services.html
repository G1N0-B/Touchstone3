Class Types

Choose the pace that suits you today. You can switch between classes at any time.

Von Newman Gentle Yoga

Slow, easy movements with plenty of rest. Ideal for beginners, older adults, and anyone returning to movement.

Vinyasa Shiva Flow

Poses linked with the breath in a steady, energizing rhythm. A good fit for busy professionals who want to build strength and release stress.

Limit Break Restorative Yoga

Supported, quiet poses held with props such as bolsters and blankets to help your body and mind unwind.

Private Sessions

Prefer one-on-one guidance? Private sessions are tailored to your goals, schedule, and comfort level.

  • A personal plan for your first weeks of practice
  • Adjustments for injuries, mobility, or limited time
  • In-studio sessions of 60 minutes
